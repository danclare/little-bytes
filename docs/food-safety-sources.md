# Food-safety sources and product boundary

This application offers general food information and family organisation, not
medical advice, allergy diagnosis or a personalised nutrition plan. Parents and
carers should check current guidance, package labels and a child's individual
care plan. Speak with a GP or health visitor when there is a diagnosed allergy,
eczema or family allergy history, feeding difficulty, swallowing concern,
poor growth or another medical need. Severe allergic symptoms are an emergency;
call 999.

Food descriptions and preparation notes are deliberately conservative. They
emphasise readiness around six months, appropriate textures, supervision,
common allergens and choking hazards. Recipe and planning suggestions are not
claims of nutritional completeness. The catalogue does not replace
professional advice or a clinician-directed exclusion diet.

## Official UK guidance

- [NHS Best Start in Life: Weaning](https://www.nhs.uk/best-start-in-life/baby/weaning/)
  — when to begin complementary feeding and how to introduce foods.
- [NHS: Foods to avoid giving babies and young children](https://www.nhs.uk/baby/weaning-and-feeding/foods-to-avoid-giving-babies-and-young-children/)
  — includes honey, whole nuts, salt, egg handling and other age-specific
  cautions.
- [NHS: Food allergies in babies and young children](https://www.nhs.uk/baby/weaning-and-feeding/food-allergies-in-babies-and-young-children/)
  — introducing allergenic foods, when to seek advice and emergency context.

These links were checked during the rebuild. Guidance can change; the NHS
pages are the source of truth. The application does not scrape or reproduce
their full text.
